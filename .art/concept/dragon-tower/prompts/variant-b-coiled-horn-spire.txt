Variant identity: coiled-horn spire. The tower body is a tapered charred stone column wrapped by a pair of long curved dragon horns that spiral up around it from the base to the crown like a coiled dragon tail, mirroring the HellfireDemon reference's horn silhouette. At the crown, the two horn tips frame a single open socket holding one large glowing ember core (like a dragon's eye turned into the tower's turret) — this glowing core is the point the fire shots launch from. The body between the coiled horns is faceted dark basalt blocks with two or three thin vertical glowing cracks running straight down, echoing a rib cage. The base is a wide, slightly asymmetric ring of broken charred rock plates pressed flat into the ground, no legs, no moss, no vegetation.
