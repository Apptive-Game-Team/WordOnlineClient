Variant identity: dragon-skull turret. The crown of the tower is a squat charred dragon skull facing forward, its jaw hinged wide open — the open jaw is the muzzle the fire shots launch from, with a bright glowing orange ember visible deep inside the mouth. Two short curved horns sweep back from the skull, echoing the HellfireDemon reference's horn shape. The mid body below the skull is a waisted charred stone column with two to three horizontal armor-plate ridges wrapping around it like a ribcage, each ridge seamed with a thin glowing orange crack. The wide base is rough broken basalt blocks with a couple of small spike shards jutting outward near the ground, no moss, no vegetation, no legs or feet — it is a built structure, rooted into the ground like a stone tower foundation.
