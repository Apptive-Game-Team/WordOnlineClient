Variant identity — "Wind spiral": one chunky rounded boulder sits at the centre-right of the canvas, built from eight to ten large warm stone facet planes with two or three small flat moss patches on its upper-left planes. Two broad pale wind ribbons wrap around the boulder in a shallow helix, passing in front of it on the lower side and behind it on the upper side, each ribbon a single long flat plane of even width with hard straight-cut ends — folded paper streamers, not vapour. The ribbon ends flare open behind the rock on the left. The rock stays fully visible between the ribbons and remains the largest mass on the canvas.
