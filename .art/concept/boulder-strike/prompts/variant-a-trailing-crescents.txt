Variant identity — "Trailing crescents": one chunky boulder, roughly egg-shaped and tilted slightly nose-down to the right, fills the right two thirds of the canvas, built from eight to ten large stone facet planes with clear hard creases between them and two or three small flat moss patches with a couple of leaf shapes on its upper-left planes. Behind it and to the left, three big crescent-shaped wind planes sweep backwards from the rock like curved blades, the nearest one largest and the furthest smallest, each a single flat pale plane with a hard edge and clear transparent space between them. The crescents are clearly separate from the rock and do not touch it.
