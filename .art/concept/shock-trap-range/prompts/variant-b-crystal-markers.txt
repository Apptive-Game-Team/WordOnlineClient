Variant identity — "Buried crystal markers": eight faceted lightning crystal shards are driven into the ground at even spacing around the ellipse, each one a small angular gold spike leaning slightly outward, half buried so its base disappears into the soil, and each sitting on its own small cracked pad of pale cream wood. Between neighbouring shards, one single thin World Tree root runs along the ground as a low flat ribbon of wood, thinner than the shards and interrupted by transparent gaps, so the crystals and not the root carry the shape. The centre of the ellipse is entirely empty.
