Variant identity — "Creeping root ring": thick World Tree roots crawl outward along the ground and form a broken ellipse, drawn as six to eight large separate root segments, each a few flat wood-coloured facet planes with hard creases, laid end to end with clear transparent gaps between them. Each segment tapers at both ends like a root tip and presses into the ground. Where two segments nearly meet, a small faceted lightning crystal chip is half buried in the soil, five or six of them around the whole ellipse, each one bright gold and much smaller than a root segment. A few small green leaves sprout at two or three points along the roots. The centre of the ellipse is entirely empty.
