Variant identity — "Split ground seam": the ellipse is marked by the ground itself being cracked open in a ring, drawn as six to eight separate angular cracks of dark brown soil, each a narrow wedge of two or three flat facet planes, arranged end to end around the ellipse with transparent gaps between them. A thin seam of bright gold lightning crystal glints inside two or three of the cracks, flush with the ground rather than rising above it, and pale cream World Tree roots reach across one or two of the cracks like stitches. The centre of the ellipse is entirely empty.
