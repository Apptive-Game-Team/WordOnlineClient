Variant identity — "Orb and beam": a faceted orb of packed energy sits left of centre, built from about a dozen large flat planes, a yellow core with a paler yellow rim on the upper left and a deeper yellow shadow band on the lower right. Out of the right side of the orb one thick straight beam fires toward the right — a solid tapering wedge of two or three flat planes, widest where it leaves the orb and narrowing to a point just short of the right edge of the canvas. Behind the orb on the left, two large chunky green chevrons curve inward toward it, each one a single flat plane with a hard edge, showing allied life being pulled in. Exactly four shapes on the canvas: orb, beam, and two chevrons.
