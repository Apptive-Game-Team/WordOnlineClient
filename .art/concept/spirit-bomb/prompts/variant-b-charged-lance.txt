Variant identity — "Charged lance": the gathered energy is drawn as one long tapering lance pointed to the right, stretching across most of the canvas — a thick faceted wedge built from four or five flat yellow planes, blunt and wide at its left end and narrowing to a sharp point at the right. A brighter pale-yellow faceted core sits inside the blunt left end, a few large planes, where the energy is densest. Two large green chevrons, each a single flat plane, fold inward against the blunt left end of the lance from above and below, showing allied life being packed in. Exactly four shapes on the canvas: lance, core, and two chevrons. This silhouette is much wider than it is tall.
