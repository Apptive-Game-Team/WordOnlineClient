Piece identity — "Tail": the last stretch of the tail and its tail fin, and nothing else.

- The body enters at the left edge of the canvas as a clean straight vertical cut, flat top to bottom, at full body thickness. From that cut the tail runs to the right and tapers smoothly to a point.
- At the right end the tail opens into one broad flat tail fin: a single chunky paddle of two or three facet planes, angled up and back, in the same blues as the body. It is a solid paddle, not a wispy fan, not a fish tail split into thin rays.
- Proportion, required: call the thickness of the body at the left cut one unit. The piece is about 2.4 units long from the left cut to the far tip of the fin, and about 1.6 units tall.
- Two or three short angular triangular fin plates continue along the top of the tapering tail, getting smaller toward the tip.
- The pale belly colour runs along the underside as a flat band.
- No head, no face, no eye, no snout, no coils, no limbs. Nothing to the left of the flat cut.
