Variant identity — "Rising coils": the serpent is reared up. Its head is at the upper right of the canvas, raised high on a thick neck and facing right with the snout slightly open, ready to fire. Below the neck the body gathers into two chunky stacked coils that fill the lower half of the canvas, the near coil overlapping the far one with one hard crease between them, each coil built from five or six large facet planes. The tail end tucks behind the lower coil and is not drawn as a separate thin point. The dorsal fin plates run up the back of the neck to just behind the head. This silhouette is taller than it is wide and should read as a big creature towering over the units around it.
