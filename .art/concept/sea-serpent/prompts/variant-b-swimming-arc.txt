Variant identity — "Swimming arc": the serpent is stretched out in one broad horizontal S-curve across the canvas, head at the right facing right with the snout slightly open, body sweeping left and the blunt tail paddle at the far left. The body is thick and even in width for most of its length — closer to a heavy log than a whip — and tapers only at the very last segment before the tail paddle. Two broad flat pectoral fins, each a single facet plane, sit just behind the head. The dorsal fin plates run the whole length of the spine. This silhouette is wider than it is tall and should read as the creature swimming forward.
