Variant identity — stacked seed-pod totem:

A short stack of two rounded, faceted seed-pod shapes (built from the same layered-facet language as the WorldTreeSpirit's body, but with no face and no eyes — these are inert carved pods, not a creature), the lower pod wider than the upper one, resting directly on the ground. The pods are bound together and to the ground by a few thick wood roots at the base. The top pod is cracked open along its upper facets, and from that crack grows a small tight bundle of glowing pale-green new shoots, like fresh regrowth sealing a wound. Keep the overall shape squat and rounded rather than a tall post, and keep the crack-and-new-growth detail as the single brightest, highest-contrast area of the image so it reads first.
