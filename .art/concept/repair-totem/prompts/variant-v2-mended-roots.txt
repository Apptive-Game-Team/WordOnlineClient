Variant identity — root-bound stone mended by living wood:

A low, wide mound built from a cracked pale stone core, visibly bound and held together by thick living roots and vines that cross over the cracks like stitches, exactly the way a splint holds a broken branch together — this sells "repair" as its concept. Warm soft green light glows out from between the root-stitches over the cracks, not from a separate gem. A small cluster of the WorldTreeSpirit-style layered leaves grows from the top where the roots gather into a knot. No face, no eyes, no limbs. Silhouette is a wide low dome rather than a tall post, clearly distinct from a tree or a creature.
