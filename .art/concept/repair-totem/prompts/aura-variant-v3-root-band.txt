Variant identity — a woven root band:

One continuous flat band of pale wood, like a low woven root hoop pressed into the ground, built from a few long facet planes with hard creases where the band bends around the circle, in the warm pale wood tones of the attached WorldTreeSpirit reference. Three or four small flat leaf facets in the nature greens sprout from the band at wide intervals. The band is closed and unbroken all the way around. No bark texture, no grain lines, no knots, no floating particles.
