Variant identity — a ring of large flat leaf facets:

Leave clear transparent gaps between the leaves so the ring breathes and the ground shows through. No stems, no vines, no swirls, and no small scatter leaves or floating dots anywhere — the ring is exactly these twelve shapes and nothing else.
